# International SEO

**Position (truthful):** "India-based technology company serving businesses worldwide." No overseas offices are claimed anywhere.

**Decision: no country pages now.** `/international/usa`, `/uk`, etc. would be near-duplicates without country-specific projects, pricing, regulation or case studies, which is the doorway pattern search engines penalise.

**What serves international visibility instead**
- One English site targeting `en` globally; schema `areaServed` = India + remote delivery.
- Service pages state remote delivery to international clients (FAQ on AI page).
- hreflang is not needed (one language version). Add `en-IN`/`en-US` only if distinct localized pages (currency, case studies) are ever created.

**Revisit when:** MyLoginn has at least one real client case study in a target country and can publish unique content (time zones, compliance, pricing in local currency).
